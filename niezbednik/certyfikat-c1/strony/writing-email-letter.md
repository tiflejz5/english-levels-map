# Email i letter – Part 2

Email lub list w Part 2 może być **formalny** (do organizatora kursu, dyrektora, redakcji) albo **nieformalny** (do znajomego). Polecenie zawsze mówi, co masz zrobić – np. **ocenić, poskarżyć się, uzasadnić i zarekomendować** działanie. Każdy z tych punktów musi się znaleźć w tekście.

## Formalny czy nieformalny?

| | Formalny | Nieformalny |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Powitanie | *Dear Mr Taylor, / Dear Sir or Madam,* | *Hi Chris, / Dear Anna,* |
| Pierwsze zdanie | *I am writing to express my concern about…* | *Great to hear from you!* |
| Styl | pełne formy, strona bierna, słownictwo formalne | skróty, phrasal verbs, pytania do czytelnika |
| Zakończenie | *I look forward to hearing from you. / Yours sincerely,* (gdy znasz nazwisko) */ Yours faithfully,* (gdy nie znasz) | *Take care, / All the best, / Speak soon,* |

## Struktura emaila formalnego (skarga + rekomendacja)

| Akapit | Zawartość |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Cel listu i kontekst (jaki kurs, kiedy). |
| 2 | Ocena ogólna – co było dobre (zrównoważony ton). |
| 3 | Skarga – co nie zgadzało się z opisem, z konkretnymi przykładami. |
| 4 | Rekomendacja – co organizator powinien zrobić. |
| 5 | Krótkie zakończenie. |

## Zwroty

| Funkcja | Zwroty |
| --- | --- |
| Cel | *I am writing with regard to… / I am writing to draw your attention to…* |
| Ocena pozytywna | *Overall, the course was well organised and… / I was particularly impressed by…* |
| Skarga | *However, I was disappointed to find that… / Contrary to what was stated in the brochure, … / This fell far short of what had been promised.* |
| Uzasadnienie | *This was particularly frustrating as… / As a result, participants were unable to…* |
| Rekomendacja | *I would strongly recommend that the publicity be revised… / It would be advisable to… / I believe a partial refund would be appropriate.* |
| Zakończenie | *I trust you will give this matter your attention. / I look forward to your reply.* |

## Wzór

*Polecenie: Kurs nie zapewnił wszystkiego, co obiecywały materiały reklamowe. Napisz email do organizatora: oceń kurs, poskarż się na nieścisłości w reklamie, uzasadnij swoje zdanie i zarekomenduj działanie.*

> Dear Ms Collins,
>
> I am writing with regard to the two-week Digital Photography course I attended at your centre in July. While there was much to enjoy, I feel I should draw your attention to several discrepancies between the course and the way it was advertised.
>
> Overall, the course was well organised. The tutor, Mark Evans, was extremely knowledgeable, and the practical sessions in the city centre were both enjoyable and genuinely useful. I returned home with a far better understanding of composition and lighting.
>
> However, I was disappointed to find that several key elements described in your brochure were missing. The publicity promised "individual feedback on every assignment", yet we received only general comments addressed to the whole group. Moreover, the brochure stated that the course would include training in professional editing software. In reality, this amounted to a single forty-minute demonstration, which was hardly enough to learn the basics. As several participants had chosen the course specifically for this reason, the omission was particularly frustrating.
>
> I would therefore recommend that the brochure be revised so that it accurately reflects what the course offers. Alternatively, if the editing module is to remain in the description, it should be extended to at least one full day. I also believe that a partial refund for this year's participants would be a fair gesture.
>
> I trust you will give this matter your attention and look forward to hearing from you.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Jan Kowalski

**Dlaczego to działa:** wszystkie cztery punkty polecenia (ocena, skarga, uzasadnienie, rekomendacja), zrównoważony ton, konkretne przykłady, subjunctive *recommend that the brochure be revised*, formalne zakończenie.

## Typowe błędy

- ❌ *Dear Sir, I write to you because…* → ✅ *I am writing with regard to…*
- ❌ *Yours faithfully* po *Dear Ms Collins* → ✅ *Yours sincerely* (znasz nazwisko).
- ❌ Agresywny ton (*This is unacceptable! I demand my money back!*) → ✅ stanowczy, ale uprzejmy.
- ❌ Brak rekomendacji – to osobny punkt polecenia.
- ❌ W liście do znajomego: zbyt formalnie (*I would like to inform you…*) → ✅ *Just wanted to let you know…*
