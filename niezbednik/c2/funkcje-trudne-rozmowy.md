# Trudne rozmowy, mediacje i negocjacje

Na C2 radzisz sobie w sytuacjach, w których liczy się każde słowo: przekazujesz złą wiadomość, rozmawiasz z pracownikiem o zwolnieniu, łagodzisz konflikt w zespole, negocjujesz twardo, ale bez palenia mostów. Sztuka polega na tym, żeby być **jednocześnie jasnym i empatycznym**.

## Przekazywanie złych wiadomości / zwolnienie

| Etap | Zwroty |
| --- | --- |
| Zapowiedź | *I'm afraid I have some difficult news. / This isn't an easy conversation to have.* |
| Jasna decyzja | *The decision has been made to let you go. / Unfortunately, your position is being made redundant.* |
| Powód (krótko) | *This is due to the restructuring of the department.* |
| Empatia | *I realise this must come as a shock. / I understand this is hard to hear.* |
| Kolejne kroki | *HR will talk you through the next steps. / We'll provide a reference and support.* |
| Przestrzeń | *Take your time. Is there anything you'd like to ask?* |

**Zasada:** nie ukrywaj decyzji pod warstwą łagodzenia. Jeśli po rozmowie osoba nie wie, czy została zwolniona – rozmowa się nie udała.

## Życie i egzamin

| Z życia | W stylu egzaminu | Egzamin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *I'm afraid we have to let you go.* (Obawiam się, że musimy się z tobą rozstać.) | Wybierz bardziej taktowną wersję zwolnienia: a) You are fired. b) You're out of the team. c) We've decided to end your contract. d) You failed. → **c** | C1 |
| *I see where you're coming from.* (Rozumiem, skąd to wynika.) | Wybierz najlepszą reakcję na złość klienta: a) Calm down. b) I understand you. c) It's not my problem. d) I see where you're coming from, and I'm sorry for the delay. → **d** | C1 |
| *I've noticed that on a few occasions the report was late.* (Zauważyłem, że kilka razy raport był spóźniony.) | Wybierz uwagę do pracownika, która unika oskarżenia: a) You never finish reports on time. b) You always send them late. c) I've noticed that deadlines have been missed on a few occasions. d) You're lazy. → **c** | C1 |

## Mediacja w konflikcie

| Cel | Zwroty |
| --- | --- |
| Neutralne otwarcie | *I'd like to hear both sides. Let's try to understand what happened.* |
| Parafraza | *What I'm hearing is that you felt left out of the decision. Is that right?* |
| Uznanie emocji | *I can see why that would be frustrating.* |
| Wspólny grunt | *It sounds like you both want the project to succeed.* |
| Szukanie rozwiązań | *What would a good outcome look like for you? / What could we do differently next time?* |
| Uzgodnienie | *So can we agree that from now on…?* |

## Twarde negocjacje – z wyczuciem

| Sytuacja | Zwroty |
| --- | --- |
| Odrzucenie bez zamykania drzwi | *That's a non-starter for us, but I'm sure we can find another way.* |
| Nacisk | *We'd need to see some movement on price before we can commit.* |
| Granica | *That's as far as we can go. / This is our bottom line.* |
| Gra na czas | *Let me take that back to my team and come back to you.* |
| Ratowanie relacji | *I appreciate your flexibility. / I'm confident we can make this work for both sides.* |

## Feedback krytyczny – model SBI

**Situation – Behaviour – Impact**

> *In yesterday's client meeting (S), you interrupted the client several times (B). I think it made them feel unheard, and they seemed less open afterwards (I). How did you see it?*

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *You are fired.* – w praktyce brzmi brutalnie → ✅ *We've decided to let you go.*
- ❌ *I understand you.* → ✅ *I understand how you feel. / I see where you're coming from.*
- ❌ nadmierne łagodzenie złej wiadomości – odbiorca nie rozumie, co się stało.
- ❌ *You always… / You never…* w konflikcie → ✅ *I've noticed that on a few occasions…*

## Ćwiczenie

Przeformułuj zdania tak, by były jasne, ale taktowne.

1. Your work is bad and you have to improve.
2. You two need to stop arguing.
3. We won't pay that much. No way.
4. You didn't get the promotion.
5. Your idea is wrong.

## Przykładowe odpowiedzi

1. I'd like to talk about some areas where I think your work could be stronger.
2. I can see you both feel strongly about this. Let's try to find a way forward together.
3. I'm afraid that's beyond what we can offer – we'd need to see some movement on price.
4. I'm afraid I have some disappointing news – the promotion has gone to another candidate. I'd like to talk about what we can do to get you there next time.
5. I see where you're coming from, but I have some concerns about how it would work in practice.
