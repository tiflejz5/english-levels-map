# Was / were – „być” w przeszłości

To pierwszy krok do mówienia o przeszłości. Z *was / were* powiesz, gdzie byłeś, jak było i jaki ktoś był – bez znajomości całego Past Simple.

## Budowa

| Osoba | Twierdzenie | Przeczenie | Pytanie |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | I **was** | I **wasn't** | **Was** I…? |
| he / she / it | she **was** | she **wasn't** | **Was** she…? |
| you / we / they | they **were** | they **weren't** | **Were** they…? |

*wasn't* = *was not*, *weren't* = *were not*

**Krótkie odpowiedzi:** *Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. / Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.*

## Typowe połączenia

- *There was / There were* – był / były (*There was a problem. There were many people.*)
- *I was born in…* – urodziłem się w… (po angielsku zawsze z *was/were*!)
- *How was…?* – jak było…? (*How was your weekend?*)
- Słowa-sygnały: *yesterday, last week, last year, two days ago, in 2010*

## Przykłady

| Angielski | Polski |
| --- | --- |
| I was at home yesterday. | Wczoraj byłem w domu. |
| Where were you last night? | Gdzie byłeś wczoraj wieczorem? |
| The film was great! | Film był świetny! |
| We weren't tired. | Nie byliśmy zmęczeni. |
| Was it expensive? – No, it wasn't. | Czy to było drogie? – Nie. |
| I was born in 1995. | Urodziłem się w 1995 roku. |
| There were five people in the room. | W pokoju było pięć osób. |
| How was your trip? | Jak minęła podróż? |

## Życie i egzamin

| Z życia | W stylu egzaminu | Egzamin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *I was at home yesterday.* (Wczoraj byłem w domu.) | Uzupełnij: Where ___ you last night? → **were** | E8 · MP |
| *The film was great, but we weren't tired.* (Film był świetny, ale nie byliśmy zmęczeni.) | Wybierz: My parents ___ at work at nine o'clock. a) was b) is c) were → **c** | E8 |
| *I was born in Gdańsk.* (Urodziłem się w Gdańsku.) | Popraw błąd: I born in Gdańsk in 2012. → **I was born in Gdańsk in 2012.** | E8 |

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *I born in Gdańsk.* → ✅ *I was born in Gdańsk.*
- ❌ *You was late.* → ✅ *You were late.* – *you* zawsze z *were*, także w liczbie pojedynczej.
- ❌ *Did you be at home?* → ✅ *Were you at home?* – z *to be* nie używamy *did*.
- ❌ *It was many people.* → ✅ *There were many people.*

## Ćwiczenie

Wpisz *was, were, wasn't* lub *weren't*.

1. I ___ at work on Monday.
2. ___ you at the party? – Yes, I ___.
3. My parents ___ in Spain last summer.
4. The weather ___ good – it rained all day.
5. Where ___ your sister born?
6. There ___ two cats in the garden.
