# Zdania rozszczepione (cleft sentences)

W polskim akcent przesuwasz szykiem: „To **Jan** to zrobił”, „Potrzebuję **czasu**”. W angielskim szyk jest sztywny, więc do podkreślenia używa się konstrukcji, która „rozszczepia” zdanie na dwie części.

## It-cleft – podkreślenie jednego elementu

**It is / was + podkreślany element + who / that + reszta**

| Zwykłe zdanie | Cleft | Co podkreślamy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| John broke the window. | ***It was John who** broke the window.* | kto (nie ktoś inny) |
| I need your help. | ***It's your help that** I need.* | czego potrzebuję |
| We met in Paris. | ***It was in Paris that** we met.* | gdzie |
| He called on Monday. | ***It was on Monday that** he called.* | kiedy |

## Wh-cleft – zapowiedź i puenta

**What + podmiot + czasownik + is / was + najważniejsza informacja**

| Zwykłe zdanie | Cleft |
| --- | --- |
| I need a holiday. | ***What I need is** a holiday.* |
| The price worries me. | ***What worries me is** the price.* |
| She called the police. | ***What she did was (to) call** the police.* |
| The car broke down. | ***What happened was (that)** the car broke down.* |

## Inne konstrukcje tego typu

| Konstrukcja | Przykład |
| --- | --- |
| All… is / was | ***All I want is** a bit of peace.* (Chcę tylko…) |
| The thing is… | ***The thing is**, I don't have time.* |
| The reason (why)… is that… | ***The reason I'm calling is that**…* |
| The person who… | ***The person who** helped me most **was** my teacher.* |
| The place where… | ***The place where** I feel happiest **is** the mountains.* |
| What I mean is… | ***What I mean is**, we need more data.* |

## Przykłady

| Angielski | Polski |
| --- | --- |
| It wasn't me who told her. | To nie ja jej powiedziałem. |
| What I really love about this city is the food. | Najbardziej w tym mieście kocham jedzenie. |
| What you should do is talk to your manager. | Powinieneś po prostu porozmawiać z szefem. |
| All we can do now is wait. | Teraz możemy tylko czekać. |
| It was only when I got home that I noticed. | Dopiero w domu zauważyłem. |
| The reason I left was the salary. | Odszedłem z powodu pensji. |

## Życie i egzamin

| Z życia | W stylu egzaminu | Egzamin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *What I really need is a holiday.* (Naprawdę potrzebuję wakacji.) | Transformacja: I need some sleep. WHAT → ___ some sleep. → **What I need is** | MR · B2 · C1 |
| *It wasn't me who told her.* (To nie ja jej powiedziałem.) | Wybierz: It was Anna ___ broke the window. a) which b) who c) what d) whose → **b** | MR · B2 · C1 |
| *All I want is some peace and quiet.* (Chcę tylko trochę spokoju.) | Uzupełnij: All she wanted ___ to go home. → **was** | MR · B2 |

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *What I need it is time.* → ✅ *What I need is time.* – bez dodatkowego *it*.
- ❌ *It was John which…* → ✅ *It was John **who / that**…*
- ❌ *All what I want…* → ✅ *All I want…* / *All that I want…*
- ❌ *The reason is because…* → ✅ *The reason is that…*

## Ćwiczenie

Przekształć zdania, zaczynając od podanych słów.

1. I need a long sleep. → What…
2. Maria found the keys. → It was…
3. Their attitude annoys me. → What…
4. I only want to be happy. → All…
5. We first met at a concert. → It was…
6. He forgot to lock the door. → What happened was…

## Odpowiedzi

1. What I need is a long sleep.
2. It was Maria who / that found the keys.
3. What annoys me is their attitude.
4. All I want is to be happy.
5. It was at a concert that we first met.
6. What happened was (that) he forgot to lock the door.
