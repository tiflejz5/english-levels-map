# Markery dyskursu

Markery dyskursu to słowa i zwroty, które **porządkują wypowiedź**: sygnalizują dodanie, kontrast, przyczynę, zmianę tematu. Na B1 używasz *however, so, because*. Na C1 masz szerszy repertuar, a Twoja wypowiedź brzmi płynnie i logicznie.

## Według funkcji

| Funkcja | Markery |
| --- | --- |
| Dodanie | *What's more, On top of that, Not to mention, Besides, Moreover (form.), Furthermore (form.)* |
| Kontrast | *Having said that, That said, Then again, Even so, Nevertheless, Nonetheless* |
| Ustępstwo | *Admittedly, Granted, To be fair, Of course* |
| Przyczyna i skutek | *That being the case, As a result, Consequently, Hence, Therefore* |
| Zmiana tematu | *On a related note, Speaking of which, Incidentally, By the way, Moving on* |
| Doprecyzowanie | *In other words, That is to say, What I mean is, To put it simply* |
| Przykład | *For instance, Take… for example, A case in point is…* |
| Podkreślenie | *In fact, As a matter of fact, Indeed, Above all* |
| Podsumowanie | *All in all, To sum up, At the end of the day, In short, Ultimately* |

## Życie i egzamin

| Z życia | W stylu egzaminu | Egzamin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Having said that, I understand your concerns.* (Mimo to rozumiem twoje obawy.) | Wybierz: The flat is cheap. ___, it isn't very close to the centre. a) What's more b) Having said that c) Moreover d) In addition → **b** | MR · B2 · C1 |
| *What's more, it saves time.* (Co więcej, to oszczędza czas.) | Uzupełnij: The plan is not perfect, but ___ the end of the day, it works. → **at** | MR · B2 |
| *On a related note, have you seen the report?* (W nawiązaniu do tego, widziałeś raport?) | Wybierz marker dodawania kolejnego argumentu: a) On the other hand, b) What's more, c) However, d) On the contrary, → **b** | MR · B2 · C1 |

## Mowa vs pismo

| Mowa (luźniej) | Pismo (formalniej) |
| --- | --- |
| *Anyway, …* | *Returning to the main point, …* |
| *Plus, …* | *Furthermore, …* |
| *Still, …* | *Nevertheless, …* |
| *So, …* | *Therefore, …* |
| *Mind you, …* (BrE) | *However, it should be noted that…* |

## Przykładowa wypowiedź

> *The new office is fantastic – it's bright, modern and close to the station. **Having said that**, it's quite expensive. **Admittedly**, we'll save on travel costs, but **even so**, the rent is 30% higher. **That being the case**, I think we should negotiate. **On a related note**, has anyone heard back from the landlord? **All in all**, I'm in favour, but not at any price.*

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *On the other hand* przy dodawaniu kolejnego argumentu (to marker kontrastu!) → ✅ *What's more, …*
- ❌ *Moreover* w luźnej rozmowie – brzmi jak wypracowanie.
- ❌ *At the end* (= w końcu) → ✅ *In the end / At the end of the day*
- ❌ *Besides of that* → ✅ *Besides that / Besides, …*

## Ćwiczenie

Wybierz najlepszy marker.

1. The hotel was great. (Having said that / What's more), the food was disappointing.
2. It's raining. (That being the case / Incidentally), let's stay inside.
3. He's not very experienced. (Granted / Hence), but he learns fast.
4. I love living here. (On a related note / As a result), do you know any good restaurants nearby?
5. The flat is cheap, spacious and (not to mention / even so) close to the centre.
6. (All in all / By the way), it was a successful year.

## Odpowiedzi

1. Having said that
2. That being the case
3. Granted
4. On a related note
