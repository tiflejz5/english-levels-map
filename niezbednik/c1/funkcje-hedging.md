# Hedging – ostrożne formułowanie tez

Hedging to celowe osłabianie pewności wypowiedzi. Nie chodzi o niezdecydowanie, tylko o **precyzję**: mówisz dokładnie tyle, ile wiesz. W pracy, nauce i dyskusjach to oznaka dojrzałości – kategoryczne stwierdzenia łatwo podważyć.

## Narzędzia

| Kategoria | Przykłady |
| --- | --- |
| Czasowniki modalne | *may, might, could, would* – *This **may** lead to problems.* |
| Czasowniki wrażenia | *seem, appear, tend to, suggest, indicate* – *The data **suggests** that…* |
| Przysłówki | *possibly, probably, arguably, presumably, apparently, generally* |
| Ograniczenie zakresu | *to some extent, to a certain degree, in some cases, largely, partly* |
| Przybliżenia | *about, around, roughly, approximately, just over, nearly* |
| Zwroty wstępne | *It would seem that…, There is some evidence that…, One could argue that…* |
| Perspektywa | *In my experience…, As far as I know…, From what I've seen…* |

## Od pewności do ostrożności

| Kategorycznie | Z hedgingiem |
| --- | --- |
| *Remote work increases productivity.* | *Remote work **tends to** increase productivity, **at least in some** sectors.* |
| *This is the best solution.* | *This is **arguably** the best solution.* |
| *The project failed because of poor management.* | *The project's failure **appears to have been largely** due to poor management.* |
| *Prices will rise by 10%.* | *Prices are **likely to** rise by **around** 10%.* |
| *Everyone agrees.* | ***Most** people **seem to** agree.* |

## Przykładowa wypowiedź

> *From what I've seen, the new process **seems to be working** quite well. **It would appear that** errors have dropped by **roughly** a third. **That said**, it's **probably** too early to draw firm conclusions – the results **may** look different once we scale up.*

## Nie przesadzaj

Zbyt dużo hedgingu brzmi jak brak zdania: *It might possibly perhaps be the case that…* Jedno, dwa słowa w zdaniu wystarczą.

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *It is 100% sure that…* → ✅ *It is very likely that…*
- ❌ *Maybe probably…* – podwójne osłabienie.
- ❌ *Arguably* jako „kontrowersyjnie” → ✅ *arguably* = „można by twierdzić, że” (zwykle wzmacnia tezę: *arguably the best*).
- ❌ *All Poles like…* → ✅ *Many Poles seem to like…* – unikaj uogólnień.

## Ćwiczenie

Dodaj hedging do zdań.

1. This method is better.
2. Young people don't read books.
3. The new law will reduce crime.
4. Sales fell by 15%.
5. The problem was caused by a software bug.

## Przykładowe odpowiedzi

1. This method is arguably better. / This method seems to be more effective.
2. Young people tend to read fewer books than previous generations.
3. The new law may help to reduce crime to some extent.
4. Sales fell by roughly 15%.
5. The problem appears to have been caused by a software bug.
