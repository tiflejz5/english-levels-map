# Prezentacje i trudne pytania

Na C1 potrafisz poprowadzić prezentację po angielsku z pewnością siebie – i, co trudniejsze, poradzić sobie z pytaniami z sali, także tymi niewygodnymi.

## Struktura i zwroty

| Etap | Zwroty |
| --- | --- |
| Otwarcie | *Good morning, everyone. Thanks for being here. Today I'd like to talk about…* |
| Cel | *By the end of this presentation, you'll have a clear picture of…* |
| Plan | *I've divided my talk into three parts. First…, then…, and finally…* |
| Pytania | *Feel free to interrupt if anything's unclear. / I'll take questions at the end.* |
| Przejścia | *Moving on to… / Let's now turn to… / That brings me to my next point.* |
| Wykresy | *As you can see from this chart, … / What's interesting here is… / This figure shows…* |
| Podkreślenie | *I'd like to stress that… / The key point here is… / What really matters is…* |
| Dygresja i powrót | *Just as an aside… / Anyway, back to…* |
| Podsumowanie | *So, to sum up… / Let me briefly recap the main points.* |
| Wezwanie do działania | *What I'd like you to take away from this is… / The next step would be…* |
| Zamknięcie | *Thank you for your attention. I'm happy to take any questions.* |

## Odpowiadanie na pytania

| Sytuacja | Zwroty |
| --- | --- |
| Kupienie czasu | *That's a good question. / Let me think about that for a second.* |
| Upewnienie się | *If I understand you correctly, you're asking… / Do you mean…?* |
| Nie znasz odpowiedzi | *I don't have the exact figures to hand, but I'll get back to you on that.* |
| Poza tematem | *That's slightly beyond the scope of today's talk, but I'm happy to discuss it afterwards.* |
| Trudne / wrogie pytanie | *I can see why you'd be concerned about that. What I can tell you is…* |
| Częściowa zgoda | *That's a fair point, and it's something we're looking into.* |
| Sprawdzenie | *Does that answer your question?* |

## Przykład odpowiedzi na trudne pytanie

> **Q:** Isn't this project just a waste of money?
> **A:** I can see why you might think that, given the costs so far. What I can tell you is that the pilot already saved us around 12% in the first quarter. I don't have the full-year projections to hand, but I'll send them round after the meeting.

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ czytanie slajdów słowo w słowo – mów do ludzi, slajd to tylko wsparcie.
- ❌ *I will tell you about…* (poprawne, ale płaskie) → ✅ *Today I'd like to walk you through…*
- ❌ *Questions?* na koniec → ✅ *I'm happy to take any questions.*
- ❌ *I don't know.* (bez niczego) → ✅ *I don't know off the top of my head, but I'll find out.*

## Ćwiczenie

Co powiesz?

1. Przechodzisz do drugiej części prezentacji.
2. Pokazujesz wykres, na którym widać wzrost sprzedaży.
3. Ktoś zadaje pytanie, na które nie znasz odpowiedzi.
4. Pytanie dotyczy tematu spoza prezentacji.
5. Kończysz i zapraszasz do pytań.

## Przykładowe odpowiedzi

1. Moving on to the second part… / That brings me to my next point.
2. As you can see from this chart, sales have risen steadily.
3. I don't have that information to hand, but I'll get back to you on that.
4. That's slightly beyond the scope of today's talk, but I'm happy to discuss it afterwards.
5. Thank you for your attention. I'm happy to take any questions.
