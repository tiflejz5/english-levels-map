# Strona bierna bezosobowa

Polskie „mówi się, że…”, „uważa się, że…”, „podobno…” mają w angielskim dwie formalne wersje. Spotkasz je w wiadomościach, raportach, tekstach naukowych – wszędzie tam, gdzie autor chce przekazać opinię lub informację z dystansem.

## Czasowniki, z którymi działa

*say, believe, think, know, consider, expect, report, claim, estimate, understand, allege, suppose*

## Konstrukcja 1 – It is said that…

**It + is / was + 3. forma + that + zdanie**

- ***It is said that** he is very rich.* (Mówi się, że jest bardzo bogaty.)
- ***It was reported that** the minister had resigned.*
- ***It is expected that** prices will rise.*

## Konstrukcja 2 – He is said to…

**Podmiot + is / was + 3. forma + bezokolicznik**

Forma bezokolicznika zależy od tego, **kiedy** dzieje się to, o czym mówimy:

| Kiedy | Forma | Przykład |
| --- | --- | --- |
| teraz / ogólnie | **to + bezokolicznik** | *He **is said to be** very rich.* |
| teraz, w trakcie | **to be + -ing** | *She **is thought to be living** in Spain.* |
| wcześniej | **to have + 3. forma** | *He **is believed to have left** the country.* |
| wcześniej, przez jakiś czas | **to have been + -ing** | *They **are reported to have been negotiating** for weeks.* |

## Porównanie obu wersji

| Czynnie | It is… that | Podmiot + to… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People say she's a genius. | It is said that she's a genius. | She is said to be a genius. |
| Police think he stole the car. | It is thought that he stole the car. | He is thought to have stolen the car. |
| Experts expect the economy to grow. | It is expected that the economy will grow. | The economy is expected to grow. |

## Przykłady

| Angielski | Polski |
| --- | --- |
| The painting is believed to be worth millions. | Uważa się, że obraz jest wart miliony. |
| It is estimated that 2 million people visited the city. | Szacuje się, że miasto odwiedziło 2 miliony osób. |
| The suspect is thought to have fled abroad. | Podejrzany prawdopodobnie uciekł za granicę. |
| She is known to be very demanding. | Wiadomo, że jest bardzo wymagająca. |
| The company is reported to be planning job cuts. | Firma podobno planuje zwolnienia. |

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *He is said that he is rich.* → ✅ *He is said **to be** rich.* / *It is said that he is rich.*
- ❌ *He is believed to leave yesterday.* → ✅ *He is believed **to have left** yesterday.*
- ❌ *It is said him to be…* → ✅ *He is said to be…*
- ❌ *They say* w raporcie → ✅ *It is said / It is believed* – brzmi bardziej obiektywnie.

## Ćwiczenie

Przekształć zdania na obie formy (It… that / podmiot + to…).

1. People say that the castle is haunted.
2. Experts believe that the fire started in the kitchen.
3. Journalists report that the singer is getting married.
4. People think that the thieves escaped through the window.

## Odpowiedzi

1. It is said that the castle is haunted. / The castle is said to be haunted.
2. It is believed that the fire started in the kitchen. / The fire is believed to have started in the kitchen.
3. It is reported that the singer is getting married. / The singer is reported to be getting married.
4. It is thought that the thieves escaped through the window. / The thieves are thought to have escaped through the window.
