# Przyczyna i przeciwieństwo

Na B2 łączysz zdania precyzyjniej niż za pomocą *but* i *because*. Kluczowa umiejętność: wiedzieć, **czy po danym słowie stoi całe zdanie, czy rzeczownik**.

## Przeciwieństwo (mimo że, chociaż)

| Słowo | Po nim | Przykład |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **although / though** | zdanie (podmiot + czasownik) | ***Although** it was raining, we went out.* |
| **even though** | zdanie (mocniejsze niż *although*) | ***Even though** he's rich, he's very modest.* |
| **despite / in spite of** | rzeczownik lub -ing | ***Despite** the rain, we went out. **In spite of** being tired, she kept working.* |
| **despite the fact that** | zdanie | ***Despite the fact that** it was raining, …* |
| **however** | nowe zdanie, po przecinku | *It was raining. **However**, we went out.* |
| **though** (na końcu, potocznie) | – | *It was raining. We went out, **though**.* |

## Kontrast między dwiema rzeczami (podczas gdy)

| Słowo | Przykład |
| --- | --- |
| **whereas** | *I love the mountains, **whereas** my wife prefers the sea.* |
| **while** | ***While** some people love it, others hate it.* |
| **on the other hand** | *It's expensive. **On the other hand**, it's very good quality.* |

## Przyczyna

| Słowo | Po nim | Przykład |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **because / as / since** | zdanie | ***As** it was late, we took a taxi.* |
| **because of** | rzeczownik | *The flight was delayed **because of** the fog.* |
| **due to / owing to** | rzeczownik (formalnie) | *The event was cancelled **due to** bad weather.* |
| **thanks to** | rzeczownik (zwykle pozytywna przyczyna) | ***Thanks to** your help, we finished on time.* |

## Przykłady

| Angielski | Polski |
| --- | --- |
| Despite his age, he runs marathons. | Mimo wieku biega maratony. |
| Although I'd never met her, I recognised her immediately. | Chociaż nigdy jej nie spotkałem, od razu ją poznałem. |
| In spite of having little money, they travel a lot. | Mimo że mają mało pieniędzy, dużo podróżują. |
| My sister is very outgoing, whereas I'm quite shy. | Moja siostra jest bardzo towarzyska, podczas gdy ja jestem raczej nieśmiały. |
| The match was postponed due to heavy snow. | Mecz przełożono z powodu obfitych opadów śniegu. |
| Since you're here, can you help me? | Skoro już tu jesteś, możesz mi pomóc? |

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *Despite of the rain…* → ✅ *Despite the rain…* / *In spite of the rain…*
- ❌ *Although it was cold, but we swam.* → ✅ *Although it was cold, we swam.* – nie łącz *although* z *but*.
- ❌ *Despite it was raining…* → ✅ *Despite the rain… / Although it was raining…*
- ❌ *Because the traffic, I was late.* → ✅ *Because **of** the traffic, I was late.*

## Ćwiczenie

Wybierz właściwe słowo.

1. (Although / Despite) feeling ill, she went to work.
2. The train was late (because / because of) a technical problem.
3. (Even though / In spite of) we left early, we missed the plane.
4. Tom loves jazz, (whereas / however) his wife can't stand it.
5. It's a great job. (Although / However), the salary is low.
6. (Despite / Although) the fact that he was tired, he finished the report.

## Odpowiedzi

1. Despite
2. because of
3. Even though
4. whereas
5. However
6. Despite
