# Łagodzenie wypowiedzi

Polacy mówiący po angielsku często brzmią zbyt bezpośrednio – nie dlatego, że są niegrzeczni, tylko dlatego, że tłumaczą wprost z polskiego. W angielskim (szczególnie brytyjskim) krytykę, niezgodę i prośby **zmiękcza się**.

## Techniki łagodzenia

| Technika | Zamiast | Powiedz |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Słowa zmiękczające | *It's expensive.* | *It's **a bit** expensive.* |
| Przeczenie pozytywnego przymiotnika | *It's bad.* | *It's **not very** good. / It's **not ideal**.* |
| Czasowniki modalne | *That's wrong.* | *That **might not be** quite right.* |
| Zwroty wstępne | *You're wrong.* | ***I'm not sure** that's entirely right.* |
| Pytanie zamiast stwierdzenia | *We should change it.* | ***Wouldn't it be better** to change it?* |
| Czas przeszły (dystans) | *I want to ask…* | *I **was wondering** if…* |
| Perspektywa osobista | *This is boring.* | ***I didn't find it** very interesting.* |
| „Obawiam się” | *No, we can't.* | ***I'm afraid** we can't.* |

## Przydatne słowa

*a bit, slightly, rather, quite, a little, somewhat, perhaps, maybe, possibly, kind of, sort of*

## Przykłady

| Bezpośrednio | Łagodniej |
| --- | --- |
| Your report has many mistakes. | There are a few small things in the report we might want to look at. |
| This idea won't work. | I'm not sure this idea would work in practice. |
| You're late again. | I noticed you were a bit late again this morning. |
| I don't like it. | It's not really my cup of tea. |
| Do it now. | Could you possibly do it now? |
| That's a stupid question. | That's an interesting question. |

## Życie i egzamin

| Z życia | W stylu egzaminu | Egzamin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *It's not really my cup of tea.* (To nie bardzo w moim guście.) | Wybierz najbardziej uprzejmą wersję krytyki: a) Your idea is bad. b) I'm not sure that idea would work in practice. c) That won't work. → **b** | MP · MR |
| *I noticed you were a bit late again this morning.* (Zauważyłem, że dziś rano znów trochę się spóźniłeś.) | Wybierz najbardziej uprzejmą wersję uwagi: a) You're always late. b) You're late again. c) I've noticed a few late arrivals recently. → **c** | MP · MR |
| *I'm afraid I can't make it on Friday.* (Obawiam się, że w piątek nie dam rady.) | Wybierz najbardziej uprzejmą odmowę: a) No, Friday is bad. b) I won't come. c) I'm afraid I can't come on Friday, sorry. → **c** | MP · MR |

## Uwaga: nie przesadzaj

Zbyt dużo łagodzenia sprawia, że komunikat ginie. Przy ważnych sprawach (bezpieczeństwo, terminy) bądź jasny: *I'm afraid this is a serious problem and we need to fix it today.*

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *You are not right.* → ✅ *I'm not sure that's quite right.*
- ❌ *I want a coffee.* → ✅ *Could I have a coffee, please?*
- ❌ *It's ugly.* → ✅ *It's not really my style.*
- ❌ *No.* (bez niczego) → ✅ *I'm afraid not.* / *Not really, no.*

## Ćwiczenie

Złagodź zdania.

1. Your presentation was too long.
2. This hotel is dirty.
3. I disagree with you.
4. Send me the file.
5. That's a bad idea.
6. We can't help you.

## Przykładowe odpowiedzi

1. Your presentation was perhaps a little long.
2. The hotel isn't very clean. / The hotel wasn't quite as clean as I expected.
3. I'm not sure I agree with you.
4. Could you send me the file, please?
5. I'm not sure that's the best idea.
6. I'm afraid we can't help you with that.
