# Negocjowanie i przekonywanie

Negocjacje to nie tylko biznes: ustalasz cenę z wykonawcą, termin z szefem, plan wakacji z rodziną. W angielskim negocjuje się **pośrednio** – przez pytania i zdania warunkowe, rzadko wprost.

## Etapy i zwroty

**1. Przedstawienie stanowiska**

- *What we're looking for is…*
- *Our main priority is…*
- *Ideally, we'd like…*

**2. Propozycje i warunki**

| Zwrot | Polski |
| --- | --- |
| If you could…, we could… | Jeśli moglibyście…, my moglibyśmy… |
| Would you be willing to…? | Czy bylibyście skłonni…? |
| How flexible are you on…? | Na ile jesteście elastyczni w kwestii…? |
| What if we…? | A gdybyśmy…? |
| We'd be prepared to… provided that… | Bylibyśmy gotowi…, pod warunkiem że… |

**3. Odrzucanie (uprzejmie)**

- *I'm afraid that's not quite what we had in mind.*
- *That would be difficult for us.*
- *I'm not sure we can go that far.*
- *That's a bit more than we were expecting to pay.*

**4. Kompromis**

- *Let's meet halfway. / Shall we split the difference?*
- *I think we can work with that.*
- *That sounds like a fair compromise.*

**5. Zamknięcie**

- *So, just to confirm: …*
- *I think we have a deal.*
- *I'll send you the details in writing.*

## Przekonywanie

- *Think about it this way…*
- *Wouldn't it make more sense to…?*
- *The advantage for you would be…*
- *You've got nothing to lose.*
- *Just imagine…*

## Przykładowy dialog

> **A:** We'd need the website finished by the end of May.
> **B:** That's quite tight. Would you be willing to extend the deadline by two weeks?
> **A:** I'm afraid that would be difficult for us. How flexible are you on the scope?
> **B:** If you could drop the online shop for now, we could deliver the rest by May.
> **A:** That sounds like a fair compromise. Let's add the shop in phase two.
> **B:** Great. I think we have a deal.

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *No, it's impossible.* – zamyka rozmowę → ✅ *That would be very difficult for us.*
- ❌ *You must give us a discount.* → ✅ *Would you be able to offer a discount?*
- ❌ *Our price is 5000 and that's all.* → ✅ *I'm afraid that's our final offer.*
- ❌ *I propose you to…* → ✅ *I'd suggest that you… / How about…?*

## Ćwiczenie

Zamień bezpośrednie zdania na negocjacyjne.

1. Give us a 10% discount.
2. No, we won't pay that.
3. Change the delivery date.
4. OK, 50/50.
5. Finish it by Friday.

## Przykładowe odpowiedzi

1. Would you be willing to offer a 10% discount?
2. I'm afraid that's a bit more than we were expecting to pay.
3. How flexible are you on the delivery date?
4. Shall we split the difference? / Let's meet halfway.
5. Would it be possible to have it finished by Friday?
