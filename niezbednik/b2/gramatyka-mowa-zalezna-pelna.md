# Pełna mowa zależna

Na B1 przekazywałeś proste zdania: *She said she was tired.* Na B2 dochodzą pytania, polecenia i – przede wszystkim – **czasowniki raportujące**. Zamiast w kółko powtarzać *said*, mówisz dokładnie, *jak* ktoś coś powiedział: przyznał, zaprzeczył, zasugerował.

## Następstwo czasów – przypomnienie

| Mowa niezależna | Mowa zależna |
| --- | --- |
| Present Simple / Continuous | Past Simple / Continuous |
| Past Simple / Present Perfect | Past Perfect |
| will / can / may | would / could / might |
| must | had to (lub must) |

Gdy informacja jest wciąż aktualna, czasu nie trzeba cofać: *He said the office **is** closed on Mondays.*

## Pytania w mowie zależnej

**Szyk twierdzący, bez do/does/did, bez znaku zapytania.**

- Pytania szczegółowe: *"Where **do you live**?"* → *She asked me where **I lived**.*
- Pytania tak/nie: *"**Are you** coming?"* → *He asked **if / whether** I was coming.*

## Polecenia i prośby

**tell / ask / order + osoba + (not) to + bezokolicznik**

- *"Sit down."* → *She **told me to sit** down.*
- *"Please don't tell anyone."* → *He **asked me not to tell** anyone.*

## Czasowniki raportujące – wzorce

| Wzorzec | Czasowniki | Przykład |
| --- | --- | --- |
| + to + bezokolicznik | agree, offer, promise, refuse, threaten | *She **refused to sign** it.* |
| + osoba + to + bezokolicznik | advise, ask, encourage, invite, persuade, remind, warn | *He **reminded me to call** Mum.* |
| + -ing | admit, deny, mention, recommend, suggest | *He **denied taking** the money.* |
| + przyimek + -ing | apologise for, insist on, accuse sb of, blame sb for, congratulate sb on | *She **apologised for being** late.* |
| + that + zdanie | admit, claim, complain, explain, insist, promise | *He **admitted that he had lied**.* |
| suggest – specjalnie | suggest + -ing / suggest that sb (should) do | *She **suggested going** out. She **suggested that we (should) go** out.* |

## Przykłady

| Mowa niezależna | Mowa zależna |
| --- | --- |
| "I didn't break it!" | He **denied breaking** it. |
| "OK, I was wrong." | She **admitted** (that) she had been wrong. |
| "Why don't we take a taxi?" | He **suggested taking** a taxi. |
| "I'll help you, if you like." | She **offered to help** me. |
| "You stole my idea!" | He **accused me of stealing** his idea. |
| "Don't forget to lock the door." | She **reminded me to lock** the door. |
| "Have you ever been to Japan?" | He asked me **if I had ever been** to Japan. |

## Życie i egzamin

| Z życia | W stylu egzaminu | Egzamin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *He denied breaking the window.* (Zaprzeczył, że stłuczył okno.) | Transformacja: "I didn't take your pen," said Tom. DENIED → Tom ___ my pen. → **denied taking** | MR · B2 |
| *She offered to help me with my luggage.* (Zaoferowała, że pomoże mi z bagażem.) | Wybierz: "Why don't we take a taxi?" – He ___ a taxi. a) suggested to take b) suggested taking c) suggested me take → **b** | MR · B2 |
| *She admitted that she had been wrong.* (Przyznała, że się myliła.) | Uzupełnij: He asked me where I ___ (work). → **worked** | MP · MR |

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *He suggested me to go.* → ✅ *He suggested (that) I (should) go.* / *He suggested going.*
- ❌ *She denied to take it.* → ✅ *She denied taking it.*
- ❌ *He asked me where do I work.* → ✅ *He asked me where I worked.*
- ❌ *He explained me the problem.* → ✅ *He explained the problem to me.*

## Ćwiczenie

Przekształć, używając czasownika w nawiasie.

1. "I'm sorry I forgot your birthday." (apologise)
2. "Let's go to the cinema." (suggest)
3. "No, I won't lend you money." (refuse)
4. "You should see a doctor." (advise)
5. "I didn't eat the last biscuit!" (deny)
6. "Where did you buy that jacket?" (ask)

## Odpowiedzi

1. He apologised for forgetting my birthday.
2. She suggested going to the cinema. / She suggested (that) we (should) go to the cinema.
3. He refused to lend me money.
4. She advised me to see a doctor.
5. He denied eating the last biscuit.
6. She asked me where I had bought that jacket.
