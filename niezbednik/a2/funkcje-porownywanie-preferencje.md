# Porównywanie i preferencje

Herbata czy kawa? Miasto czy wieś? Pytania o preferencje to idealny materiał do rozmowy – możesz porównać, wybrać i uzasadnić.

## Wyrażanie preferencji

| Zwrot | Gramatyka | Przykład |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I prefer A to B. | rzeczownik / -ing | *I prefer tea **to** coffee. I prefer walking **to** driving.* |
| I'd prefer… | + to + bezokolicznik (teraz, konkretnie) | *I'd prefer to stay in tonight.* |
| I'd rather… (than…) | + bezokolicznik bez to | *I'd rather go by train **than** fly.* |
| I like A more than B. | rzeczownik / -ing | *I like summer more than winter.* |
| A is better / nicer than B. | stopniowanie | *The city is more exciting than the countryside.* |

## Życie i egzamin

| Z życia | W stylu egzaminu | Egzamin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *I prefer tea to coffee.* (Wolę herbatę od kawy.) | Uzupełnij: I prefer swimming ___ running. → **to** | E8 · MP |
| *I'd rather stay at home tonight.* (Wolałbym zostać dziś w domu.) | Wybierz: I'd rather ___ at home. a) to stay b) staying c) stay → **c** | E8 |
| *Which do you like more, cats or dogs?* (Które lubisz bardziej, koty czy psy?) | Popraw błąd: I prefer tea than coffee. → **I prefer tea to coffee.** | E8 |

## Pytania

- *Which do you prefer, tea or coffee?*
- *Would you rather live in the city or in the countryside?*
- *What's better – working from home or in an office?*

## Uzasadnienie

Zawsze dodaj **because**:
*I prefer the train **because** it's more comfortable and I can read.*

## Przykładowy dialog

> **A:** Which do you prefer – the mountains or the seaside?
> **B:** I prefer the mountains. They're quieter than the seaside and I love hiking. What about you?
> **A:** I'd rather go to the seaside. I like swimming more than walking.
> **B:** But the Baltic is so cold!
> **A:** It's not as cold as you think!

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *I prefer tea than coffee.* → ✅ *I prefer tea **to** coffee.*
- ❌ *I'd rather to stay.* → ✅ *I'd rather stay.*
- ❌ *I more like summer.* → ✅ *I like summer more.*
- ❌ *more better* → ✅ *better*

## Ćwiczenie

Uzupełnij.

1. I prefer cats ___ dogs.
2. I'd rather ___ (stay) at home tonight.
3. Which do you ___, pizza or pasta?
4. Living in a city is ___ (expensive) than living in a village.
5. I'd rather walk ___ take the bus.
6. I'd prefer ___ (sit) by the window.

## Odpowiedzi

1. to
2. stay
3. prefer
4. more expensive
5. than
6. to sit
