# Past Continuous

Past Continuous opisuje czynność, która **trwała** w jakimś momencie w przeszłości. Najczęściej jest tłem dla krótkiego wydarzenia w Past Simple.

## Kiedy używamy

- **Czynność w toku w konkretnym momencie** – *At 8 p.m. I was having dinner.*
- **Tło + przerwanie** – *I was cooking **when** he called.* (gotowałem → zadzwonił)
- **Dwie czynności naraz** – *I was reading **while** she was watching TV.*
- **Opis scenerii w opowieści** – *It was raining and people were running home.*

## Budowa

**was / were + czasownik -ing**

| | Wzór | Przykład |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Twierdzenie | I / he / she / it **was** + -ing; you / we / they **were** + -ing | *She was sleeping.* |
| Przeczenie | **wasn't / weren't** + -ing | *We weren't listening.* |
| Pytanie | **Was / Were** + osoba + -ing? | *Were you working?* |

## When czy while?

- **when** + krótka czynność (Past Simple): *I was walking home **when** I saw him.*
- **while** + długa czynność (Past Continuous): ***While** I was walking home, I saw him.*

## Przykłady

| Angielski | Polski |
| --- | --- |
| What were you doing at 10 last night? | Co robiłeś wczoraj o 22? |
| I was watching a film. | Oglądałem film. |
| She was driving when her phone rang. | Prowadziła samochód, kiedy zadzwonił telefon. |
| We weren't sleeping, we were talking. | Nie spaliśmy, rozmawialiśmy. |
| While I was cooking, the kids were playing. | Kiedy gotowałem, dzieci się bawiły. |
| The sun was shining and birds were singing. | Świeciło słońce i śpiewały ptaki. |

## Życie i egzamin

| Z życia | W stylu egzaminu | Egzamin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *I was cooking when you called.* (Gotowałem, kiedy zadzwoniłeś.) | Uzupełnij: At eight o'clock yesterday I ___ (have) a shower. → **was having** | E8 · MP |
| *They were playing football when it started to rain.* (Grali w piłkę, kiedy zaczęło padać.) | Wybierz: What ___ you doing at eight last night? a) was b) did c) were → **c** | E8 |
| *She was sleeping when I arrived.* (Spała, kiedy przyjechałem.) | Popraw błąd: They was watching TV at nine. → **They were watching TV at nine.** | E8 |

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *When I cooked, he called.* (gdy chodzi o tło) → ✅ *When I **was cooking**, he called.*
- ❌ *I was knowing him.* → ✅ *I knew him.* – czasowniki stanu bez -ing.
- ❌ *They was playing.* → ✅ *They were playing.*
- ❌ *While he called…* → ✅ *When he called…* – *while* nie z krótką czynnością.

## Ćwiczenie

Wybierz Past Simple lub Past Continuous.

1. I ___ (read) when the lights ___ (go) out.
2. What ___ you ___ (do) at 6 p.m. yesterday?
3. While we ___ (walk) in the park, it ___ (start) to rain.
4. She ___ (not / listen) when the teacher ___ (ask) her a question.
5. They ___ (have) lunch when I ___ (arrive).

## Odpowiedzi

1. was reading, went
2. were, doing
3. were walking, started
4. wasn't listening, asked
5. were having, arrived
