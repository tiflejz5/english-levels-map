# Czasownik + -ing czy + to?

Gdy po jednym czasowniku stoi drugi, ten drugi ma formę **-ing** albo **to + bezokolicznik**. Wybór zależy od pierwszego czasownika – trzeba to zapamiętać w parach.

## Po tych czasownikach: -ing

*like, love, hate, enjoy, don't mind, finish, stop, go (+ czynność), be good at, be interested in*

- *I **enjoy cooking**.*
- *She **finished writing** the report.*
- *Let's **go swimming**.*
- *He's **good at playing** chess.*

**Po każdym przyimku zawsze -ing:** *before leaving, after eating, thanks for coming, I'm interested in learning…*

## Po tych czasownikach: to + bezokolicznik

*want, would like, need, decide, hope, plan, learn, try, forget, agree, promise, offer*

- *I **want to travel**.*
- *We **decided to stay**.*
- *I'd **like to book** a table.*
- *She **learned to drive** at 30.*

## Po czasownikach modalnych: sam bezokolicznik (bez to)

*can, could, must, should, will, would, let's*

- *I **can swim**. You **should go**. **Let's eat**.*

## Like / love / hate – obie formy

*I like swimming. = I like to swim.* (różnica minimalna). Ale ***would like*** – tylko *to*: *I'd like to go.*

## Życie i egzamin

| Z życia | W stylu egzaminu | Egzamin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *I enjoy dancing and I want to learn Spanish.* (Lubię tańczyć i chcę uczyć się hiszpańskiego.) | Uzupełnij: She wants ___ (visit) London. → **to visit** | E8 · MP |
| *I'm interested in learning languages.* (Interesuje mnie nauka języków.) | Wybierz: My dad enjoys ___ football. a) watching b) to watch c) watch → **a** | E8 |
| *I decided to stay at home.* (Postanowiłem zostać w domu.) | Popraw błąd: I can to swim very well. → **I can swim very well.** | E8 |

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *I want going.* → ✅ *I want to go.*
- ❌ *I enjoy to dance.* → ✅ *I enjoy dancing.*
- ❌ *I'm interested in learn.* → ✅ *I'm interested in learning.*
- ❌ *I can to swim.* → ✅ *I can swim.*

## Ćwiczenie

Wpisz *-ing* lub *to* + czasownik.

1. I enjoy ___ (travel).
2. She wants ___ (become) a doctor.
3. We decided ___ (stay) at home.
4. He's good at ___ (draw).
5. Have you finished ___ (eat)?
6. I'd like ___ (order), please.
7. Thanks for ___ (call).
