# Ściąga A1

## Czasy i formy

| Forma | Budowa | Przykład |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present Simple | czasownik; he/she/it + **-s**; don't / doesn't + bezokol. | *She works. Do you work?* |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are + -ing | *I'm reading. It's raining.* |
| going to | am/is/are going to + bezokol. | *I'm going to study.* |
| was / were | I/he/she/it **was**; you/we/they **were** | *Were you at home?* |
| can / can't | can + bezokol. (bez -s, bez to) | *She can drive.* |
| there is / are | There's a bank. There are two banks. | *Is there a toilet here?* |
| to be, have got | I'm / you're / she's; have / has got | *I've got two brothers.* |

## Gramatyka

- **Przedimki:** *a teacher*, *an engineer* (przed samogłoską), *the* – gdy wiadomo, o co chodzi. [[a1-gramatyka-przedimki]]
- **Liczba mnoga:** -s / -es (*box → boxes*); *child → children, man → men, woman → women, person → people, foot → feet*.
- **Przyimki:** **in** Gdańsk, in May · **on** Monday, on the table · **at** 7 o'clock, at home. [[a1-gramatyka-przyimki-miejsca-czasu]]
- **Szyk zdania:** podmiot + orzeczenie + dopełnienie: *I play tennis on Sundays.*
- **Pytania:** *Where **do** you live? What time **is it**? How **much** / How **many**?*
- **this / these** – blisko, **that / those** – daleko. [[a1-gramatyka-this-that-these-those]]
- **Zaimki:** I – me – my · he – him – his · she – her – her · we – us – our · they – them – their.
- **Pisownia:** *watch → watches, study → studies, make → making, run → running.*
