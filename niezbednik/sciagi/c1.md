# Ściąga C1

## Struktury

| Struktura | Budowa | Przykład |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inwersja | przysłówek negatywny + szyk pytający | *Never have I seen … Not only does she sing … No sooner had she left than …* |
| Zdania rozszczepione | What … is … / It was … who | *What I need is time. It was John who told her.* |
| be to / be about to | formalny plan; bliska przyszłość | *The law is to come into force. I'm about to leave.* |
| Imiesłowy | Having + 3. forma; Not + -ing | *Having lived in Spain, she speaks fluent Spanish.* |
| Warunkowe bez if | Had / Should / Were it not for | *Had we left earlier, we wouldn't have missed it.* |
| Bierna bezosobowa | It is said that … / He is said to … | *He is believed to have left.* |
| Nominalizacja | rzeczownik zamiast czasownika | *The introduction of the policy caused confusion.* |
| Czas dla grzeczności | was wondering, wanted, was hoping | *I was wondering if you could help me.* |

## Gramatyka

- **Elipsa:** *I think **so** / I hope **not*** (nie: *I think yes*); *I'd love **to***; *So am I.* [[c1-gramatyka-elipsa-substytucja]]
- **Cleft:** bez dodatkowego *it*: *What I need is time.* *All I want …* (nie: *all what*); *The reason is **that** …*
- **Inwersja:** *Not only **does she** sing …*; *Hardly had we arrived **when** …*; *No sooner … **than** …*
- **Imiesłowy:** ten sam podmiot w obu częściach: *Having finished the work, I went home.*
- **Bez if:** *Should you have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.*
- **Nominalizacja:** *a rise / an increase in prices* (nie: *increasement*).
- **Hedging:** *very likely, seem to, arguably, to some extent* zamiast *100% sure*.
- **Streszczenia i anegdoty:** Present Simple – *The film is about … he finds …*
- **Bierna bezosobowa:** *is said to be*; o przeszłości *is believed to have left*.

## Słowa-sygnały

| Słowo | Struktura |
| --- | --- |
| Never, Hardly, Seldom, Not only, No sooner | inwersja |
| What … is, It was … who, All … is | zdanie rozszczepione |
| Having + 3. forma, Not wanting … | imiesłów |
| Had, Should, Were it not for | warunkowe bez if |
| It is said / believed / estimated | bierna bezosobowa |

## Uwaga na

- ✗ Never I have seen … → ✓ **Never have I seen …**
- ✗ I think yes. / I hope no. → ✓ **I think so. / I hope not.**
- ✗ He is believed to leave yesterday. → ✓ **He is believed to have left yesterday.**
- ✗ Hardly had we arrived than … → ✓ **Hardly had we arrived when …**
- ✗ make a research → ✓ **do / conduct research**
