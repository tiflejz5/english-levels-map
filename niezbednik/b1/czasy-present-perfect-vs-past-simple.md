# Present Perfect czy Past Simple?

Po polsku oba czasy tłumaczymy zwykle tak samo, dlatego Polacy często je mylą. Pytanie kontrolne jest jedno: **czy okres, o którym mówisz, już się zamknął?**

## Zasada główna

| Past Simple | Present Perfect |
| --- | --- |
| okres **zamknięty**, wiadomo kiedy | okres **otwarty** (aż do teraz) lub czas nieistotny |
| *I lived in Gdańsk for 5 years.* (już tam nie mieszkam) | *I've lived in Warsaw for 5 years.* (nadal mieszkam) |
| *I saw him yesterday.* | *I've seen this film.* (kiedy – nieważne) |
| *She wrote three books.* (nie żyje / skończyła) | *She's written three books.* (nadal pisze) |

## Słowa-sygnały

| Past Simple | Present Perfect |
| --- | --- |
| yesterday, last week, ago, in 2019, when I was a child, What time…? When…? | for, since, ever, never, just, already, yet, so far, recently, this week (jeszcze trwa), How long…? |

## For i since

Z Present Perfect mówimy, od jak dawna coś trwa (po polsku czas teraźniejszy!):

- **for** + okres: *for three years, for a long time, for ages*
- **since** + punkt startu: *since 2020, since Monday, since I was a child*
- *I've known her for 10 years.* – Znam ją od 10 lat.
- *How long have you worked here?* – Od jak dawna tu pracujesz?

## Typowy schemat rozmowy

Pytanie ogólne w Present Perfect → szczegóły w Past Simple:

> **A:** Have you ever been to Japan?
> **B:** Yes, I have. I went there in 2018.
> **A:** Really? How long did you stay?
> **B:** Two weeks. It was amazing.

## Przykłady

| Angielski | Polski |
| --- | --- |
| I've worked here since 2021. | Pracuję tu od 2021 roku. |
| I worked there from 2015 to 2020. | Pracowałem tam w latach 2015–2020. |
| Have you seen my keys? I can't find them. | Widziałeś moje klucze? Nie mogę ich znaleźć. |
| Did you see the match last night? | Widziałeś wczoraj mecz? |
| We've been married for 12 years. | Jesteśmy małżeństwem od 12 lat. |
| She's lost her phone. (nadal go nie ma) | Zgubiła telefon. |

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *I live here for 3 years.* → ✅ *I've lived here for 3 years.*
- ❌ *I know him since school.* → ✅ *I've known him since school.*
- ❌ *When have you arrived?* → ✅ *When did you arrive?* – *when* = Past Simple.
- ❌ *I've been there last year.* → ✅ *I was there last year.*

## Ćwiczenie

Wybierz Present Perfect lub Past Simple.

1. I ___ (know) Marek since we were kids.
2. She ___ (move) to Berlin in 2017.
3. ___ you ever ___ (try) skiing?
4. When ___ you ___ (start) learning English?
5. We ___ (live) here for six years and we love it.
6. Oh no! I ___ (lose) my keys and I can't get into the house.

## Odpowiedzi

1. have known
2. moved
3. Have, tried
4. did, start
5. have lived
6. have lost ('ve lost) – skutek jest teraz, czas nie jest podany
