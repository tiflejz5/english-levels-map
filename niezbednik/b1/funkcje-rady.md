# Udzielanie rad i proszenie o radę

Rada w angielskim zwykle jest „opakowana” – zamiast „Zrób to” mówi się „Może byś…?”. Im słabsza relacja z rozmówcą, tym delikatniej.

## Proszenie o radę

- *What should I do?*
- *What would you do (if you were me)?*
- *Do you have any advice?* (*advice* niepoliczalne!)
- *Can I ask your advice about something?*
- *Do you think I should…?*

## Udzielanie rady (od najmocniejszej)

| Zwrot | Gramatyka | Przykład |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You'd better… | + bezokolicznik bez to | *You'd better see a doctor.* (ostrzeżenie) |
| You should / ought to… | + bezokolicznik | *You should take a break.* |
| If I were you, I'd… | + bezokolicznik | *If I were you, I'd talk to her.* |
| Why don't you…? | + bezokolicznik | *Why don't you try yoga?* |
| How about / What about…? | + -ing | *How about asking your manager?* |
| Have you thought about…? | + -ing | *Have you thought about changing jobs?* |
| It might be a good idea to… | + bezokolicznik | *It might be a good idea to save some money.* |
| Maybe you could… | + bezokolicznik | *Maybe you could call him.* |

Odradzanie: *You shouldn't… / I wouldn't… if I were you. / You'd better not…*

## Reakcja na radę

- *That's a good idea. Thanks!*
- *Maybe you're right.*
- *I've already tried that.*
- *I'm not sure that would work, because…*

## Przykładowy dialog

> **A:** I can't sleep at night. What should I do?
> **B:** Have you thought about stopping coffee after lunch?
> **A:** I've already tried that.
> **B:** Hmm. If I were you, I'd stop using my phone in bed. And why don't you try going for a walk in the evening?
> **A:** That's a good idea. I'll try it.

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ *Can you give me an advice?* → ✅ *Can you give me some advice / a piece of advice?*
- ❌ *You should to go.* → ✅ *You should go.*
- ❌ *You had better to…* → ✅ *You'd better…*
- ❌ *Why you don't try…?* → ✅ *Why don't you try…?*

## Ćwiczenie

Uzupełnij.

1. If I ___ you, I'd apologise.
2. Why ___ you ask for a discount?
3. How about ___ (take) a day off?
4. You'd better ___ (not / be) late again.
5. Can I ask your ___ about something?
6. You ___ eat so much sugar. (rada negatywna)

## Odpowiedzi

1. were
2. don't
3. taking
4. not be
5. advice
6. shouldn't
