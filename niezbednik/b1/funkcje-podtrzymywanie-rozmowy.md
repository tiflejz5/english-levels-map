# Utrzymywanie rozmowy

Rozmowa to nie przesłuchanie ani monolog – to ping-pong. Na B1 potrafisz pokazać, że słuchasz, reagować i zadawać pytania, które ciągną temat dalej.

## 1. Aktywne słuchanie – krótkie reakcje

| Zwrot | Kiedy |
| --- | --- |
| Really? / Seriously? | zdziwienie |
| That's interesting! / Wow! | zainteresowanie |
| Oh no! / That's terrible! | współczucie |
| That's great! / Good for you! | radość za kogoś |
| I see. / Right. / Uh-huh. | „słucham, mów dalej” |
| I know what you mean. | zrozumienie |

## Życie i egzamin

| Z życia | W stylu egzaminu | Egzamin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Really? And what did you do then?* (Naprawdę? I co wtedy zrobiłeś?) | Wybierz reakcję: – I've just moved to a new city. a) Yes. b) Oh, really? How do you like it? c) Fine. → **b** | E8 · MP |
| *Oh, I see. That sounds interesting!* (Aha, rozumiem. Brzmi ciekawie!) | Wybierz: – I went to Spain last summer. a) Did you? Where exactly? b) Do you? c) Have you? → **a** | E8 · MP |
| *Have you? Where was that?* (Naprawdę? Gdzie to było?) | Wybierz: – I've never been to London. a) Haven't you? b) Don't you? c) Aren't you? → **a** | MP · MR |

## 2. Pytania zwrotne (echo)

Krótkie pytanie z czasownikiem posiłkowym – bardzo naturalne:

- *I've just come back from Mexico.* – ***Have you?** How was it?*
- *I don't eat meat.* – ***Don't you?***
- *My sister lives in Australia.* – ***Does she?***
- *We were in Paris last week.* – ***Were you?***

## 3. Pytania follow-up – ciągnij temat

- *What happened next?*
- *How did you feel about that?*
- *What was the best part?*
- *Why did you decide to…?*
- *What's it like?*
- *How long have you been doing that?*

## 4. Odbijanie piłeczki

- *What about you? / And you?*
- *How about you – have you ever…?*

## 5. Zmiana tematu i powrót

- *By the way, … / Speaking of…, …*
- *Anyway, … / So, …*
- *Going back to what you said about…*

## Przykładowy dialog

> **A:** I started rock climbing last month.
> **B:** Did you? That's cool! What made you try it?
> **A:** A friend took me, and I loved it.
> **B:** Really? Isn't it scary?
> **A:** A bit at first, but you get used to it.
> **B:** I can imagine. Where do you go?
> **A:** There's an indoor wall near my office. Have you ever tried it?
> **B:** No, never. But speaking of sport, I've just signed up for a half-marathon!

## Typowe błędy Polaków

- ❌ odpowiedzi jednym słowem (*Yes. Fine. No.*) – zawsze dodaj zdanie i pytanie zwrotne.
- ❌ *Yes?* jako echo → ✅ *Are you? / Did you? / Have you?*
- ❌ cisza przy słuchaniu – po angielsku oczekuje się sygnałów (*Right, I see, Really?*).
- ❌ *And you?* po każdym zdaniu – urozmaicaj pytania.
